# Editorial Guidelines

## Verification Standards

Every story in Veeranjali must be verified through reliable sources. We distinguish between different levels of verification:

### Verification Levels

1. **Officially Documented** - Verified through official government or military records
2. **Verified Multiple Sources** - Confirmed by two or more reliable independent sources
3. **Family Submitted** - Information provided by immediate family members
4. **Community Submitted** - Information from community members with supporting documentation
5. **Under Review** - Currently being verified
6. **Information Incomplete** - Some details are missing or unverified

## Source Requirements

### Accepted Sources

- Official government records (military, police, government publications)
- Published news articles from reputable news organizations
- Published books from recognized publishers
- Academic publications and research papers
- Official organization websites and archives
- Family records with appropriate supporting documentation

### Source Documentation

Every source must include:
- Title of the source
- Publisher or author
- Publication date (if available)
- URL or physical location
- Date accessed
- Notes on reliability

## Privacy Rules

### Information We Do NOT Publish

- Private residential addresses
- Personal phone numbers or email addresses
- Government identification numbers (Aadhaar, PAN, etc.)
- Bank or financial information
- Names of minor children
- Graphic photographs or descriptions
- Information that could compromise operational security
- Unverified personal details

### Family Information

Family information is only included when:
- It is already publicly documented, OR
- Explicit consent has been provided by the family
- Names of surviving family members are included only with consent

## Use of Terminology

### Respectful Terminology

We use respectful terminology appropriate to the circumstances:

- **"Died in service"** - General term for death during service
- **"Killed in action"** - Death during combat operations
- **"Martyred"** - When this term is officially used or family preference
- **"Lost their life during an operation"** - Alternative phrasing

### Terminology Selection

The selected wording must be:
- Supported by an authoritative source, OR
- Aligned with family preference, OR
- Appropriate to the documented circumstances

## Correction Process

### Submitting Corrections

Anyone can submit a correction report:
1. Click "Report an error" on any profile
2. Provide a clear description of the error
3. Include a supporting source URL if available
4. Submit the report

### Review Process

1. Admin reviews the report
2. Verifies the suggested correction
3. Updates the profile if appropriate
4. Marks the report as resolved

## Handling Disputed Information

When information is disputed:
1. We clearly label it as disputed
2. Include all credible perspectives
3. Cite sources for each claim
4. Update as new information becomes available

## Incomplete Biographies

When biographical information is incomplete:
1. Clearly label missing sections
2. Include only verified information
3. Note what is being researched
4. Accept community contributions for missing details

## Photo and Document Attribution

### Requirements

All media must include:
- Clear attribution (photographer, source, or archive)
- Alt text for accessibility
- Caption describing the content
- License information if applicable

### Approval

Media is only published after:
- Verification of ownership/rights
- Review for appropriate content
- Approval by editorial team

## Family Submissions

### Process

Families can submit:
1. Biographical details
2. Photographs
3. Documents
4. Corrections

### Requirements

- Verification of family relationship
- Supporting documentation where possible
- Consent for publication

## Independence

Veeranjali is an independent project. We:
- Are not affiliated with any government agency
- Are not affiliated with any political party
- Maintain editorial independence
- Follow journalistic standards
